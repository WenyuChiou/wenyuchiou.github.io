§ 02 Research Experience

  • Ph.D. Candidate — Complex Adaptive Water Systems (CAWS) Lab, Lehigh University Aug 2024 – present

    • Co-authored the Water Resources Research (2026) article substituting large language models as the behavioral engine of a human–water simulation — published; second author of two.
    • Building the Water Agent Governance Framework (WAGF) with validation rules, execution control, and audit logs — agent decisions are checked against physical, financial, and behavioral-theory constraints before simulation-state updates; research software in preparation for release.
