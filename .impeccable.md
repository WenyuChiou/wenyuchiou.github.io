## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters for AI-research / engineering internships (Summer 2027), academic peers (water-resources / ABM / LLM-agent researchers), and ML engineers. Context: a ~30-second credibility scan, usually desktop-first, sometimes mobile. The site is a recruiting + scholarly identity surface.

### Brand Personality
**Precise · scholarly · humane.** The work is governed LLM agents reasoning about flood / catastrophe risk and human decision-making — rigor and verification on one side, people and living water systems on the other. Voice: an exhibit caption in a serious museum — calm, exact, quietly confident, never loud or salesy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial / magazine (KEEP): serif display headline with a restrained accent + italic, small-caps labels, generous whitespace, asymmetric two-column hero, first-class light + dark themes (OKLCH tokens). Distinctive typography is the signature.
Anti-references / "AI slop" to avoid: Inter / Fraunces monoculture, cyan-on-dark, purple-to-blue gradients, neon, gradient text, repeated card-grid sameness, sparkline-as-decoration.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography carries the brand** — distinctive, non-default faces (Spectral display + Hanken Grotesk body + JetBrains Mono); strong hierarchy (≥1.25 scale), tight tracking on display, comfortable body leading; body line length ~70ch.
2. **Whitespace with rhythm, not dead gaps** — vary vertical spacing to signal hierarchy instead of one uniform pad.
3. **Accent is rare and meaningful** (60-30-10) — the teal "signal" marks the research through-line; violet is structural; no decorative color.
4. **Credibility over flash** — real artifacts (the WRR paper, FLOODABM, repos, data viz) lead; motion is one tasteful page-load, not scattered micro-interactions.
5. **Both themes first-class; never pure black / white.**
