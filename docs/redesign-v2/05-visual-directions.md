# Visual Directions

## Direction A — Behavioral Systems Observatory (selected)

- Metaphor: a scientific instrument for following a decision through evidence, model, governance, and feedback.
- Typography: Literata for English editorial argument and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for English interface text; Chiron Sung HK and Chiron Hei HK for Traditional Chinese.
- Color: high-contrast paper white and ink, signal teal, coral validity risk, amber decision state.
- Layout: full-bleed AGU image with a solid translucent reading field, unframed expertise blocks, editorial work rows, and expandable evidence stages.
- Motion: native detail expansion and short state transitions only; reduced-motion compliant.
- Risk: the instrument language could feel impersonal, so a real first-viewport photograph and direct first-person copy remain central.

## Direction B — Field Research Atlas

- Metaphor: a field notebook combining survey, map, and hydrology layers.
- Treatment: topographic lines, map fragments, Source Serif, earth/water colors, marginal annotations.
- Strength: clearly tied to human–environment systems.
- Risk: could over-position Wenyu as a flood-only researcher and read as beige/earth-tone editorial styling.

## Direction C — Constraint Ledger

- Metaphor: an audit ledger for agent proposals, failed rules, repairs, and accepted transitions.
- Treatment: denser mono metadata, red/green rule states, strict tabular layouts.
- Strength: strong governance and engineering signal.
- Risk: too operational for a personal research homepage; weaker human presence.

## Selection Criteria

Direction A won on identity fit, cross-audience clarity, evidence legibility, mobile feasibility, static fallback, and originality. It evolves the strongest part of the previous editorial design while removing its identity toggle and card-heavy hierarchy.

## 2026-08 Capability-Led Revision

- The hero and expertise section now establish LLM behavior evaluation within five seconds.
- Selected Work appears before the detailed observatory and leads with role, problem, and method.
- Numeric proof strips, repeated evidence-ledger content, glass treatments, gradients, monospace labels, colored top rules, and equal-height card grids are excluded from the homepage.
- Flood and human–environment systems remain visible as consequential testbeds, not as the sole professional label.
