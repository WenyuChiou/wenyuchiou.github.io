# Positioning Brief

## Final Positioning

**Ph.D. Researcher specializing in Human-Grounded LLM Behavior Evaluation.**

LLM behavior evaluation is the center of the public story. Governed agent systems and behavioral simulation show how Wenyu carries evaluation beyond generated text into inspectable actions and system-level consequences.

## Hero Message

**I evaluate how LLM agents make decisions, build systems that govern their actions, and model how those decisions unfold in complex environments.**

The homepage introduces capabilities rather than study scale. Quantitative evidence remains available in case studies, publications, and the four audience-specific CVs.

## Language Rules

- Lead with the specialty and the problems Wenyu can solve, not study size or AI tooling.
- Use psychometrics, CFA, and SEM as methods after the visitor understands the question.
- Say `compare`, `correspond`, `align`, or describe the measured relationship directly.
- Keep `published`, `under revision`, `in preparation`, `research prototype`, and `maintained open source` visible.
- Keep `937`, `52,141`, and `50 runs` out of the homepage introduction and LinkedIn About.
- Treat industry and academic emphasis as PDF document choices, never a global site identity switch.

## Deliberate Exclusions

No tool-icon wall, fake AI demo, terminal motif, model leaderboard, unpublished numeric result, or claim that a model can replace a person.
