# Positioning Brief

## Final Positioning

**Ph.D. Researcher in Human-Grounded LLM Evaluation, Governed Agents, and Behavioral Simulation.**

The public story is one continuous system: measured human decisions inform pathway models; pathways inform simulation and LLM evaluation; governance controls whether an action can update an external system; the external system returns physical and financial feedback.

## Hero Message

**Human-grounded AI agents for behavioral and human–environment simulation.**

Supporting proof: 937 human profiles, 52,141 simulated households, 50 runs per FLOODABM scenario, a 2026 WRR publication, and 6K+ open-source community proof.

## Language Rules

- Lead with the research problem and evidence, not AI tooling.
- Use psychometrics, CFA, and SEM as methods after the visitor understands the question.
- Say `compare`, `correspond`, `align`, or describe the measured relationship directly.
- Keep `published`, `under revision`, `in preparation`, `research prototype`, and `maintained open source` visible.
- Treat industry and academic emphasis as PDF document choices, never a global site identity switch.

## Deliberate Exclusions

No tool-icon wall, fake AI demo, terminal motif, model leaderboard, unpublished numeric result, or claim that a model can replace a person.
